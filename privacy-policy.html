Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Tiruttani Quick

1. Information We Collect

Tiruttani Quick collects necessary personal information when you register, browse products, or place orders through our website or mobile application:

  • Account Details: Name, phone number, and email address.
  • Delivery Coordinates: Address, city (Tiruttani, Tamil Nadu), and location pincode.
  • Transaction Data: Items ordered, order history, billing totals, and delivery timestamps.
  • Device Data: App usage metrics, FCM notification tokens, and device identifiers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for service delivery purposes:

  • Fulfilling grocery orders and delivering items to your address in Tiruttani.
  • Sending real-time order status updates via push notifications and SMS.
  • Providing customer support and processing refunds or delivery inquiries.
  • Improving service reliability and catalog availability in local delivery zones.

3. Data Security & Storage

Your data is stored securely in Firebase cloud infrastructure with strict security rules. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers.

4. Account Deletion & Rights

You have the full right to request deletion of your account and personal data at any time. Visit our dedicated Account Deletion Page to submit a deletion request.
