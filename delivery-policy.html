Delivery Policy

Local Coverage & Delivery Terms • Tiruttani Quick

1. Service Area & Local Coverage

Tiruttani Quick provides dedicated local grocery delivery throughout Tiruttani town and supported nearby residential areas in Tamil Nadu, India. Delivery is performed by our local delivery fleet.

2. Delivery Fee Structure

  • Orders ₹500 & Above: FREE Local Delivery across supported Tiruttani zones.
  • Orders Below ₹500: Standard nominal delivery fee of ₹29.

3. Real-Time Delivery Status

Our shop delivery availability status is synced in real time between our central operational dashboard and customer platforms:

  • 🟢 Delivery Available: Orders are actively accepted and dispatched.
  • 🔴 Delivery Currently Unavailable: Store delivery is temporarily paused (e.g., during store maintenance or severe weather). Customers can still browse the product catalog.

4. Delivery Inspection

Upon receiving your delivery package in Tiruttani, please inspect the items with the delivery agent. If any item is missing or damaged, inform the agent or contact helpline +91 96980 46731 immediately.
