Refund & Return Policy

Customer Satisfaction Promise • Tiruttani Quick

1. Damaged or Incorrect Products

At Tiruttani Quick, product quality is our top priority. If you receive a damaged package, expired product, or incorrect item, please notify our helpline at +91 96980 46731 or email Ranukastore@gmail.com within 24 hours of delivery.

2. Return Eligibility

  • Non-perishable packaged groceries must be unused and in original packaging.
  • Damaged or defective items are eligible for immediate replacement or full refund.
  • Perishable items (like milk or fresh produce) should be reported upon delivery inspection.

3. Order Cancellations

You can cancel an order free of charge before the store packs and dispatches your delivery. Once an order is out for delivery, cancellations may be subject to a nominal handling fee.

4. Refund Processing Timeline

Approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method or UPI account within 3 to 5 business days. For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are issued via direct UPI transfer.
